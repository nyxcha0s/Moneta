# Moneta

I built Moneta because every budgeting app assumes you have your shit together already. They want you to categorize your lattes and set savings goals. That's not useful when you're starting from zero.

Moneta is built around one real question: I have this much money right now, these bills are due on these dates, what do I actually do? It tracks paycheck to paycheck reality, helps decide what gets paid now versus what gets pushed, and is designed to eventually help crawl out of survival mode into actually getting ahead.

No judgement. No avocado toast lectures. Just math and a plan.

**Built with:** Python, Flask, SQLite, Claude API, systemd

**What it does:**
- Tracks real available money against real due dates
- Models paycheck to paycheck cash flow
- Helps prioritize which bills get paid in which order
- Designed for people starting from zero, not people optimizing abundance

**Status:** Active development.
